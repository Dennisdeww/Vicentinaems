Технология EMS

Электромиостимуляция (EMS) — это революционная тренировка для всего тела, использующая безопасные низкочастотные электрические импульсы для одновременной стимуляции до 90% мышц. Этот метод обеспечивает высокоинтенсивную тренировку всего за 20 минут, что делает его невероятно эффективным способом достижения ваших фитнес-целей.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EMS training safe?

Absolutely. EMS is a well-researched technology that has been used in physiotherapy for decades. Our certified instructors ensure the impulses are set to your comfort level and supervise every session for maximum safety and effectiveness.

Does the impulse hurt?

No, it doesn't hurt. You'll feel a unique tingling or buzzing sensation as your muscles contract. The intensity is always adjusted to your personal comfort and fitness level. Most people find it to be a new and interesting feeling.

How can 20 minutes be enough?

The magic of EMS is its efficiency. It activates up to 90% of your muscles simultaneously, including deep, hard-to-reach muscle fibers. This high level of muscle engagement makes the workout incredibly intense, delivering results comparable to several hours of conventional gym training.

How often should I train?

For optimal results, we recommend one or two 20-minute sessions per week. Because the workout is so intense, your body needs time to recover and rebuild muscle. This recovery period is crucial for seeing progress.

Will I see results quickly?

Many clients report feeling stronger and more energized after just a few sessions. Visible results, like improved muscle tone and fat reduction, typically become noticeable within 4 to 8 weeks when combined with a balanced lifestyle. Consistency is key!
